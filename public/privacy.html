Privacy Policy

Business Analysis over WhatsApp · last updated 23 September 2026

This is a demonstration project. A business owner sends a question over WhatsApp and receives an analysis of fictional sales data, with a chart. This page explains what happens to the information involved.

What we receive

When you message the service, WhatsApp (Meta) delivers to us your phone number and the text of your message. We do not receive your contacts, your profile, media, location or any other conversation.

How it is used

Your message is used only to answer that same message. The question is sent to an AI model provider to generate the analysis, the chart is rendered by QuickChart, and the answer is sent back to you through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Your phone number is used only to address the reply.

What we store

We do not keep a history of conversations. Each message is processed and then discarded; the service has no memory of earlier questions. Operational logs kept by our hosting provider may briefly contain technical details of a request, and are not used for any other purpose.

What we share

We do not sell or share your information. It passes only through the providers needed to answer: Meta (WhatsApp), the AI model provider, Netlify (hosting) and QuickChart (charts). The sales data the service reads is fictional and contains no personal data about you.

Your choices

You can stop using the service at any time by no longer sending messages. Because we do not store conversations, there is nothing kept to delete; if you want confirmation, ask through the same WhatsApp number.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
